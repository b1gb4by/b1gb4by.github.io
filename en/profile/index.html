Long Version (~200 words)#

Taisuke Okamoto is a Developer Experience & Performance Engineer at PLAID, Inc., where he focuses on development infrastructure, CI/CD automation, and developer experience improvements for the KARTE platform. He is also actively involved in OLAP database development, building and maintaining distributed analytical systems on Google Kubernetes Engine with deep integrations to BigQuery, Cloud Storage, and Spanner. Prior to joining PLAID, he served at IDC Frontier where he led the development of a Kubernetes as a Service offering on CloudStack, implementing CSI Drivers, Cloud Controller Managers, and Ingress Controllers. He also designed and delivered a comprehensive three-month container and Kubernetes education program for engineers company-wide, consistently achieving over 90% satisfaction ratings. Before that, at Video Market, he spearheaded the migration of a video streaming platform from PHP to Go with a Kubernetes-based microservices architecture and designed multi-DRM systems supporting Widevine, PlayReady, and FairPlay. He actively contributes to the cloud-native community as Co-chair of CloudNative Days, Japan's largest cloud-native tech conference, and as Co-Organizer of Grafana Meetup Japan, where he promotes observability practices using the LGTM stack and related technologies. He is also active as a DJ and VJ at nightclubs and music festivals in Tokyo.
